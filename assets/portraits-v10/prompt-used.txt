Generation direction for
work/generated-art-v10/family-portrait-atlas-v10.png:

Create one coherent 3 by 2 pixel-art family portrait atlas for a warm narrative
game. Top row: the same adult caregiver in Sunrise, Meadow, and Starlight
appearance presets. Bottom row: the same 9-10-year-old child in the matching
three presets. Every cell is a head-and-upper-torso three-quarter portrait
facing right toward dialogue, at one camera distance, scale, outline weight,
and lighting direction. Keep full hair silhouettes and chins inside each cell.
Faces must remain clean and human: natural warm skin ramps, neutral eye whites,
dark readable irises, no cyan or blue skin, no split-color faces, and no
favorite-color tint on facial features. Use crisp deliberate pixel clusters,
selective dark outlines, one shadow ramp and one highlight ramp, with no text,
frame, props, scenery, blur, or extra figures. Separate cells with generous
gutters on a flat chroma-magenta background.

scripts/process-ui-v10.mjs extracts each portrait to a common 384 by 384 RGBA
canvas. The five child favorite-color variants are deterministic edits of only
the lower backpack region; their face, eyes, skin, hair, jacket, and identity
remain unchanged.

Generation direction for

Create one coherent 4 by 2 atlas of warm pixel-art dialogue portraits for the

icon, and one unused decorative placeholder. Use one head-and-shoulders crop,

chroma spill, zeros transparent RGB, and exports every result on the same
384 by 384 RGBA canvas.
