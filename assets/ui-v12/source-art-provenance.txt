V12 uses image-generated decorative source art with deterministic geometry.
All player-facing text is live DOM text; no generated text is used.

Mode: Built-in image generation plus deterministic asset extraction.

Generation direction for work/generated-art-v10/ui-component-atlas-v10.png:

Create one original, text-free pixel-art UI component atlas for a warm,
child-centered healthcare journey game. Use large readable shapes inspired by
cozy story RPG interfaces without copying any existing game's layout,
ornament, characters, or symbols. The atlas must include a wide dialogue
frame, separate portrait frame, nameplate, wide and compact buttons, one
choice-card frame, a three-part journey-status frame, a compact status strip,
and an information card. Keep generous uninterrupted text-safe interiors.
Visual language: honey-colored wood, warm cream paper, turquoise and coral
inlays, restrained leaf and stitched-route details, deep navy-brown outlines,
crisp intentional pixel clusters, and consistent corner treatment. No words,
letters, numbers, icons that resemble text, screenshots, scenes, characters,
blur, gradients, or soft drop shadows. Arrange separated components on a flat
chroma-magenta background with clear extraction gutters.

scripts/process-ui-v10.mjs crops the generated components, removes only
edge-connected chroma pixels plus exact chroma remnants, trims transparent
padding, and exports RGBA PNGs. setup-panel.png, menu-panel.png,
story-ribbon.png, and menu-button.png retain the approved earlier
image-generated art because those source panels already match the same
text-free production direction. All visible labels and controls remain live
DOM text so names, keyboard focus, screen readers, and localization cannot
corrupt or stretch generated decoration.

Staff animation strips: Codex built-in image generation, 2026-08-08.
Each existing in-game staff sprite was used as the identity, clothing, palette,
proportion, and facing-direction seed. Complete four-pose walk, idle/blink,
and calm speaking cycles were generated per role as 2-by-2 grids on flat
chroma-magenta backgrounds. The prompts required alternating arms and feet for
walking; planted feet, a natural blink, and restrained hand/mouth gestures for
conversation; one shared footline and scale; crisp pixel clusters; no scenery,
text, shadows, or extra characters. The chroma-key helper removed the
background; scripts/normalize-generated-staff-walk.mjs then applied one scale
per strip and a shared bottom-center anchor. The shipped staff-*-walk.png files
under public/assets/staff-v11/ sit beside their matching idle and talk strips.

Dialogue staff portraits: Codex built-in image generation, 2026-08-09.
The approved family portrait was used as the pixel-density, face-scale, warm
palette, and three-quarter-facing style reference. Each previous role portrait
and, where available, its matching in-scene staff sprite supplied only the
identity, clothing, and role details. Six separate prompts produced the
clinician, patient navigator, pediatric cardiologist, test technician,
children's-center phone staff member, and scheduler on flat chroma-magenta
plates with no scenery, text, UI, or shadows. The prompts required every face
to look right toward the dialogue copy and prohibited cropped hair, shoulders,
cyan skin, blue eye corruption, blur, and fine anti-aliased rendering.
scripts/normalize-generated-portrait.mjs removes edge-connected chroma and
magenta spill, clears RGB under transparency, uses nearest-neighbour scaling,
and applies the shared 384px content area plus 24px safe padding. Rebuildable
normalized masters live in art-source/ui-v12/portraits/; the six shipped
portraits under public/assets/ui-v12/ are direct deterministic copies.

The caregiver, child, and narrator portraits are also copied byte-for-byte
from their approved 432px V11 masters. The V12 build must not resize or
palette-quantize those files: changing a favorite-color accent is never
allowed to alter a face, skin tone, eye, hair, or clothing pixel.
