Generation direction for work/generated-art-v10/staff-stage-atlas-v10.png:

Create one coherent pixel-art stage-character atlas with four distinct caring
healthcare workers: neighborhood clinician, patient navigator, pediatric
cardiologist, and pediatric test technician. Give each role a stable,
recognizable identity that matches its dialogue portrait and cannot be mistaken
for another role. Show an idle source pose and a talking source pose for each
person at the same full-body camera distance, body scale, outline weight,
lighting, and feet line. Characters face left toward the family, use warm
professional expressions, age-appropriate proportions, role-readable clothing,
clean continuous silhouettes, natural skin and eye colors, and crisp intentional
pixel clusters. No split-color faces, cyan skin, text, labels, UI, props,
scenery, floor shadows, blur, or extra figures. Arrange the eight separated
cells on a flat chroma-magenta background with wide extraction gutters.

scripts/process-staff-v10.mjs extracts the four role columns and two motion
rows, removes chroma, normalizes every figure onto 128 by 128 frames with the
y=121 footline, and assembles deterministic four-frame idle and talk sheets.
scripts/process-role-portraits-v10.mjs also takes the dialogue bust for each
in-person role from this exact atlas so clothing, face, hair, and identity match
the worker standing in the scene. The role-specific outputs must remain
separate; the runtime must never replace them with one generic staff identity.
