Mode: Deterministic production derivation from the approved image-generated
family motion masters in public/assets/sprites-hd.

The approved masters were generated as dedicated idle, talk, reaction, and
walk sheets for one caregiver and one 9-10-year-old child. Each sheet preserves
one identity, fixed camera, centerline, body scale, and feet line; uses complete
full-body three-quarter silhouettes facing right; and keeps motion to readable
connected pixel-cluster changes. The intended rendering is crisp handcrafted
pixel art with no text, UI, props, scenery, blur, or antialiasing.

scripts/build-sprite-v10.py applies explicit appearance ramps for Sunrise,
Meadow, and Starlight while preserving each accepted motion silhouette. It
repairs cyan contamination inside face regions, locks a consistent skin and
hair ramp per preset, and confines the child's five favorite colors to the
outer backpack accent mask. Favorite color is not permitted to recolor eyes,
skin, hair, face shadows, or the whole character. Every exported frame remains
on the shared 128 by 128 logical grid and y=121 footline.
