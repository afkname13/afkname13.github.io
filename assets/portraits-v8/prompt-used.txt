Mode: Built-in image generation, new assets.

The staff portrait source requested one coherent 3 by 2 chroma-key sheet with
six original modern healthcare workers at the same camera distance and pixel
scale: neighborhood clinician, children's-center phone staff, scheduler,
hospital navigator, pediatric cardiologist, and pediatric test technician.
The direction required warm expressions, varied identities and skin tones,
clean continuous silhouettes, no split-color faces, no text, and no copied
characters.

The narrator source requested a square open route notebook icon with a simple
home-to-clinic-to-hospital dotted path, pencil, and page tab. It intentionally
uses an object instead of inventing a narrator character.

stage-staff.png is not a generated replacement character. It is a deterministic
nearest-neighbor portrait crop of the first 128 by 128 frame in
sprites-hd/staff-idle.png: source rectangle x=32, y=0, width=64, height=64,
scaled exactly 3x to 192 by 192, with alpha deterministically snapped at 128 to
retain hard pixel edges. This keeps the dialogue portrait identical to the
clinician, patient navigator, cardiologist, and technician actor visible in the
scene.
